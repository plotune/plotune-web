Our flagship · Plotune Nexus

One controlled surface for hardware-facing work.

Local-first by design

Runs on Plotune-defined hardware. Raw artifacts stay on the device and move only to storage you already control.

Bounded, not raw

AI-assisted work runs through a narrow set of safe, approved actions instead of direct hardware access, so you always know what an agent can and cannot do.

Repeatable evidence

Recurring capture and stimulation move from operator memory into reusable jobs and test sequences that leave a packaged artifact behind.

Works with your setup

Connects to the real test systems your benches, vehicles, and rigs already use, with broad support that ships today.
