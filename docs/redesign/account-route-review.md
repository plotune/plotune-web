## Findings

- Routes 1–21 and 23, 25, 27–29: no significant visual defects found in the opened mobile, tablet, or desktop captures. Layouts remain legible at all three widths and show no visible horizontal clipping.
- Route 22 (`/stream/prototypes/vision`): the page labels its contents as a dated “DEMO SNAPSHOT”; the sample test metrics and events are clearly presented as illustrative.
- Route 1 (`/faq`): final captures show the FAQ sections and expanded answers at all three widths; no clipping or layout issue is visible.
- Route 24 (`/partners`): the final captures show the responsive video area and a visible “Watch the partnership video on YouTube” fallback link at mobile, tablet, and desktop widths. The embedded YouTube content remains blank under safe screenshot fixtures, so playback itself was not verified. No clipping or layout break is visible.
- Route 26 (`/partners/apply`): the external Google Form embed is blank under the safe fixtures. “Open it in a new tab” and direct contact links remain visible. This is a fixture limitation.
- Route 16 (`/verify-email`): fixture arrives without a valid verification token and displays its expected invalid/expired-link state. Route 6 (`/download`) includes fixture release metadata rendered as “Invalid Date”; this is test data, not a verified live release claim.

## Static CTA and navigation audit

Source review confirms these in-app destinations in the assigned pages:

- Login: “Forgot password?” → `/reset-password`; registration link → `/register`; successful login → `/dashboard`.
- Register: legal links → `/legal` and `/privacy`; sign-in → `/login`; successful registration → `/verify-email`.
- Forgot password and verification recovery links return to `/login`; verification also links home and to support email.
- Profile links to documentation and password reset. Dashboard quick actions route to extensions, download, and streams; page quick links use their configured route destinations.
- DNS “Upgrade to Plus” → `/partners`; partner application returns to `/partners`, and its form/contact fallbacks target the external form and contact email.
- Storage recommendation links route to `/extensions` and `/streams`; stream cards link to `/streams/connect`.
- External links observed in scope include Gravatar, Google Forms, mailto contacts, and configured dashboard resources. No auth, API, or form behavior was changed during the visual redesign.

Static destination review is not an end-to-end click-through test. Production captures for this review were inspected visually only; interactive behavior remains covered by the existing project verification performed by the root agent.
