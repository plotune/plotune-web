## Connect Microsoft Copilot

Added once in Copilot Studio, usually by whoever manages your agents.

- In Copilot Studio, open your agent and choose Tools, Add a tool, New tool, Model Context Protocol.

- Paste the address below and choose OAuth 2.0 with dynamic discovery.

- Create the connection, sign in to Plotune and add it to the agent.

`https://mcp.plotune.net/mcp`

[Read the full guide](https://www.plotune.net/docs/mcp)
