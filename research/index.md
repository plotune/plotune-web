How to read this chart+

Each point is one model, colored by provider. Higher and further left is better. The dotted Pareto frontier connects models where no other model scores at least as well on both axes and strictly better on one. The shaded corner marks a strong combination of performance and cost. Cost and time are benchmark-wide averages; the workflow filter changes the validation index.
