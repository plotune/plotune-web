Review and approval

Requirements move from Draft to In review to Approved, and each change to the wording is a new revision. The person who decides sees the statement and approves or rejects it.

DrafttoIn reviewtoApproved
PC-004In reviewRev 2

The controller shall record every alarm with its time and source.

RejectApprove

Read the statement and criteria, then approve or reject.

Changing the statement or criteria of an approved requirement starts the next revision as a draft. The approved revision stays in its history.
